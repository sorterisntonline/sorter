# Sorter: The Consensus Layer for the Internet

### Sorter in one sentence

**A Layer 2 social network that plugs into any platform (starting with Reddit) to find the true consensus in any online conversation, using a powerful pairwise comparison engine to separate the signal from the noise.**

---

## 1️⃣ Why the Internet Urgently Needs a 'Sort' Button

The modern internet is an ocean of opinions, but our tools for navigating it are broken. This leads to fundamental, widespread problems.

| What We Have Today                                        | The Pain It Causes                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Simple 'Like' / 'Upvote' buttons**                      | This blunt instrument treats all preferences as equal, missing the *magnitude* of opinion. A "meh" upvote counts the same as a passionate one.                                                                                 |
| **Sorting by 'Popular' or 'Recent'**                      | Platforms reward the loudest, earliest, or most inflammatory voices, not the most insightful or representative ones. The "wisdom of the crowd" is buried.                                                                     |
| **Chaotic, N² Conversations**                             | A Reddit thread with 2,000 comments or a viral tweet's reply section is functionally unreadable. Valuable discourse is lost in a firehose of unorganized text.                                                                 |
| **Lack of Cross-Platform Identity**                       | Your taste in music on Spotify, films on IMDb, and books on Goodreads are trapped in walled gardens. There's no way to build a universal "graph" of your preferences.                                                        |
| **Inability to find consensus**                           | It is nearly impossible to tell what a community *actually* believes is the best solution or the most correct argument. Outrage mobs and echo chambers dominate, making genuine compromise and understanding feel impossible. |

---

## 2️⃣ What Sorter Gives the World

Sorter is a utility that plugs into existing content streams and provides a fundamentally new way to see them.

1.  **The Universal Ingest Engine**
    Paste a URL from Reddit, Spotify, IMDb, etc., and Sorter ingests its content (comments, tracks, movies) as entities ready to be sorted. It solves the "cold start" problem by providing immediate value on top of content people already care about.

2.  **High-Fidelity Pairwise Sorting**
    Instead of a simple upvote, users are presented with two items at a time and asked, "Which is better, and by how much?" using a `-50..+50` slider. This captures rich, nuanced preference data that simple voting misses.

3.  **True Consensus Ranking Algorithm**
    Our ranking engine doesn't just count votes. It analyzes the web of pairwise comparisons to find what ideas win the most head-to-head matchups. As proven in our own tests, this method reliably surfaces the **most broadly acceptable, middle-ground options**—the true consensus—even when they aren't the passionate first choice of any single group.

4.  **Shareable "Truth Artifacts"**
    The result isn't just a list; it's an insightful, shareable permalink that represents the collective judgment of the community. This includes compelling, gamified results like percentile rankings (e.g., "This comment is in the 95th percentile for helpfulness," or "You are a 72nd percentile asshole, according to `r/AITA` voters").

## 5️⃣ The Grand Vision: Organizing Human Opinion

The Reddit sorter is just the first step. Success there unlocks a much larger, more profound opportunity.

1.  **From Tool to Destination:** Users initially come to sort a Reddit thread, but they create a Sorter account to track their own votes. Sorter becomes the central hub for their opinions across the entire internet.
2.  **The Universal Preference Graph:** As we add ingestors for Spotify, IMDb, Goodreads, Twitter, etc., we build a single, unified graph of how humanity ranks and relates ideas, culture, and products.
3.  **The Consensus API:** Anonymized, aggregated access to this preference graph becomes an incredibly valuable resource for research, journalism, and market intelligence, providing a powerful long-term business model.
4.  **An Epistemic Public Utility:** Ultimately, Sorter aims to become a foundational layer of the internet—a public good that helps individuals, communities, and society make sense of themselves in an age of information overload.
